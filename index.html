How it works

Every generator produces the full set of commands in the order they have to run, with a copy button on each line and a Copy all at the bottom. Where ordering matters — charging a player before handing over the goods, say — the generator puts the commands in the order that actually works and explains why.

Coordinates go in one box, so you can paste a set straight out of the game or off a line in your notes. -394.74 66.00 -300.93, 607, 62, 1238 and Market: -337 64 -318 all read correctly, and decimals get rounded down to the block they sit in. Tick relative to turn the numbers into tilde offsets.

Region tools show you the size and block count, and warn you before you hit Bedrock's 32,768 block ceiling on /fill and /clone.
