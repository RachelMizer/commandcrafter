Scoreboards

Every currency, quest flag, timer and purchase counter runs on a scoreboard. Bedrock has exactly one criteria — dummy — so scores only ever change when a command changes them. That makes them reliable, and it means most of the work is in editing them rather than setting them up.

Every scoreboard command

Objectives

CommandWhat it does
objectives add <name> dummy [display] Creates an objective. dummy is the only criteria Bedrock has.
objectives remove <name> Deletes it along with every score on it. No undo.
objectives list Lists every objective that exists.
objectives setdisplay <slot> [name] [order] Shows an objective in sidebar, list or belowname. Leave the name off to empty the slot. Order is ascending or descending.

Scores

CommandWhat it does
players add <target> <obj> <n> Adds n. With n as 0, registers players without changing anything.
players remove <target> <obj> <n> Subtracts n. Scores are allowed to go negative.
players set <target> <obj> <n> Overwrites the score with n.
players reset <target> [obj] Takes them off the objective entirely — not the same as setting 0. They stop matching scores={…}. Omit the objective to clear them from all of them.
players list [target] Prints scores into chat.
players test <target> <obj> <min> [max] Reports whether a score sits in a range. * means unbounded.
players random <target> <obj> <min> <max> Sets a random value. Both ends included.
players operation <target> <obj> <op> <source> <obj> Combines two scores. Operations below.

Operations

=Copy the source score onto the target
+=Add the source to the target
-=Subtract the source from the target
*=Multiply
/=Divide, whole numbers only. Dividing by 0 fails and changes nothing.
%=Remainder after dividing
<Keep whichever score is smaller
>Keep whichever score is larger
><Swap the two scores

Ranges in selectors

scores={Coins=10..}10 or more — what a price check wants
scores={Coins=..9}9 or less — the matching failure branch
scores={Coins=5..10}Between 5 and 10, both included
scores={Coins=12}Exactly 12 — excludes anyone holding 13
scores={Coins=!0}Anything except 0
scores={Quest=1,Coins=10..}Both conditions at once

A player who has never been scored on an objective matches no range at all, not even ..0. That is why putting everyone on the board with add @a <obj> 0 is worth doing up front — otherwise a new player's first purchase silently does nothing.
